# Food types to review

The Danish names and the words that point to each food type (S-009) are drafts until the owner has checked them. Produce names come from stead, where they were checked; they are not listed here.

**What to check:**

- Is the Danish name what a Danish home calls it?
- Do the words (keys) point to the right type, and could any of them catch something else?
- The times are FoodKeeper's own and are not reviewed here (but see the judgement calls).

## Judgement calls to look at first

These map a Danish product to the nearest FoodKeeper record, which is not a perfect match:

- **Skyr** uses Yogurt (33). FoodKeeper has no skyr.
- **Remoulade, aioli, burgerdressing** use commercial Mayonnaise (350).
- **Feta, mozzarella, halloumi, blåskimmelost** use Soft cheese (7), the shortest cheese times.
- **Danbo, havarti and other skæreost** use Hard cheese (3).
- **"Pølser" on its own** uses cooked or smoked sausages (103); raw sausages (medister) have their own type.
- **"Kylling" on its own** uses raw chicken breast (117).
- **"Fisk" on its own and hellefisk** use Fatty fish (146), the shorter freezer time.
- **Sojadrik and risdrik** use Soy or rice beverage (408). There is no type for havredrik (oat drink).
- **Milk:** FoodKeeper says "package use-by date". Danish milk is usually marked "mindst holdbar til" (best before); the user picks the kind of date.
- **Hakket kalv og flæsk** uses Minced beef (43); all minced meats have the same times.
- **Quiche, tærte** is in Rester og færdigretter (FoodKeeper: 2 hours out of the fridge).
- **Tænk's freezer lines (S-014)** are matched to types in `content/danish-freezer.json`. Check: leftovers, cooked rice and soups count as "færdige retter" (1–3 months); "Frossen fisk, rå" counts as fatty fish (2–3 months), the cautious choice when the kind of fish is unknown; Tænk's "Grøntsager" and "Frugt" cover all fresh vegetables and fruit.
